Bubble, dew, lightning

如夢幻泡影，如露亦如電 — “like a dream, a phantom, a bubble, a shadow; like dew, like lightning”, the Diamond Sūtra’s closing line. Play, and the name of the moment you’re in surfaces beside the point itself.

  • 夢hold still, untouched
  • 幻drift it slowly
  • 影move it at a brisk walk
  • 泡tap, click, or press space
  • 露let go and watch it fade
  • 電flick it fast
